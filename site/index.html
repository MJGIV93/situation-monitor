          How the scores work

          Both scores run 0 to 100 and higher is worse. Every reading carries a signal from 0 to 100, which is how much concern it adds, not how big its number is. A falling stock index or a train running late has a high signal. Each reading shows its rule and arithmetic when opened. A domain takes its worst reading plus a fifth of the next two. Domains then stack by weight, so several unrelated problems at once raise a score far more than one alone. The points under each dial show how far that score would fall if the domain went quiet.

          0 to 9 is quiet, 10 to 24 watch, 25 to 49 elevated, 50 to 74 disrupted, 75 and up severe.

          Readings refresh every 10 minutes, and slower sources hourly. If a source stops answering, its last reading stays up. It is dimmed after 3 hours and stops counting after 24.
